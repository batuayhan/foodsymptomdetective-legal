Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 5, 2026

Food Symptom Detective ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Key point: Your health and food data never leaves your device. We do not operate servers that collect, store, or process your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

The App allows you to log and track the following types of data on your device:

  • Food logs -- meals, ingredients, and dining details you record
  • Symptom logs -- symptoms, severity, timing, and notes you enter
  • Wellness data -- stress levels, sleep quality, exercise activity, and menstrual cycle information

This information may be considered sensitive health data under applicable privacy laws. We treat it with the highest level of care.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All data you enter into the App is stored locally on your device using an on-device SQLite database. Specifically:

  • Your data is never transmitted to any external server, cloud service, or third party.
  • We do not maintain user accounts or authentication systems.
  • We do not have access to any data you enter into the App.
  • Your data resides solely within the App's sandboxed storage on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

RevenueCat -- We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat processes purchase transactions through Apple's App Store or Google Play. This involves:

  • An anonymous user identifier generated by RevenueCat (not linked to your personal identity)
  • Purchase and subscription status information provided by Apple or Google
  • No health data, food logs, symptom logs, or wellness data is shared with RevenueCat

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Purchase transactions are also subject to the privacy policies of Apple and Google, as applicable.

4. Analytics and Tracking

The App does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect usage statistics, crash reports, or behavioral data.

5. Notifications

The App may send you local push notifications (for example, reminders to log meals or symptoms). These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any remote notification servers. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

6. Health Data Disclaimer

Important: Food Symptom Detective is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The App identifies statistical patterns in the data you enter. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.

7. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data at all times:

  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all data associated with it.
  • You can also delete individual entries or clear all data from within the App's settings.
  • Once deleted, data cannot be recovered, as we do not maintain any backups or copies.

8. Data Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features, including device encryption, passcode or biometric locks, and the operating system's app sandboxing. Since data never leaves your device, it is not exposed to network-based security risks.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used this App, please note that uninstalling the App will remove all associated data from the device.

10. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because we do not collect or process your personal data on any server, most of these rights are inherently satisfied:

  • Right of access -- All your data is visible to you within the App at all times.
  • Right to rectification -- You can edit any entry within the App.
  • Right to erasure -- You can delete individual entries or uninstall the App to remove all data.
  • Right to data portability -- The App provides data export functionality.
  • Right to restrict processing -- All processing occurs locally on your device under your control.

No data is transferred outside your device, so there is no cross-border data transfer to disclose.

11. Your Rights Under CCPA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.
  • All data remains on your device and is fully under your control.

12. KVKK (Turkish Data Protection)

If you are a resident of Turkey, you have rights under the Kisisel Verilerin Korunmasi Kanunu (KVKK), Law No. 6698. Your health data is processed locally on your device and is not transferred to any server. You have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed
  • Request information about processing if your data has been processed
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used in accordance with its purpose
  • Know the third parties to whom your data is transferred domestically or abroad
  • Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data
  • Request deletion or destruction of your personal data
  • Object to any result that is against your interests by analysis of processed data exclusively through automated systems

Since all your health data is stored exclusively on your device and we do not collect, transfer, or process your personal health information on any server, you maintain full control over your data at all times. You can delete all your data by uninstalling the application.

For KVKK-related inquiries, contact us at: privacy@foodsymptomdetective.com
